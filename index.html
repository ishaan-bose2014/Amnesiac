Amnesiac

Wipe the save data from the games that came with your computer, and play them like the first time.

Download for macOS Download for Windows

Works with the games that ship with macOS and Windows.

How it works

  1. Open Amnesiac

    It finds the built-in games on your Mac or PC and lists them.

  2. Pick a game to forget

    Choose which saves, stats and high scores it should lose.

  3. Start over

    The game opens as if you'd never played it. Nothing else on your computer is touched.

Made by two friends who wanted to beat Solitaire again.
